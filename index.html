About the event

Tradition in every detail. Creativity in every creation.

Craft India Expo 2027 is a handicrafts trade fair bringing together talented artisans, craft makers, small businesses, designers, buyers and curious visitors under one roof.

From intricate textiles and pottery to jewellery and home décor, the expo gives makers a platform to showcase their work, meet new customers and grow their businesses.
